# OSSM Folded Sheet Metal Stand

![OSSM Folded Sheet Metal Stand](img/stand-preview.png)

A freestanding stand with a one-piece 3 mm steel frame, pivoting square-tube
arm, tube braces and wraparound feet. Designed for the **70 mm PitClamp Mini mount**.

- [FreeCAD model](cad/ossm-folded-sheet-metal-stand.FCStd) — native Sheet Metal bends and unfold.
- [STEP assembly](cad/ossm-folded-sheet-metal-stand.step) — individual parts and flat reference in [cad/](cad/).
- [Foot STLs](print/) — one per corner.
- [BOM and build notes](docs/build-notes.md).

Edit with FreeCAD 1.1.4 and Sheet Metal 0.8.23.

**Prototype:** angle-lock hardware is unfinished. Strength, stability and
full-machine folding clearance need physical testing.
