# LGBT Branded Actuator Front Body

<img src="img/front-body-preview.png" width="360" alt="OSSM front body with trans flag stripes and flush branding">

A one-piece cover and middle body with trans flag stripes and flush **OSSM.TECH** and **R+D** inlays. Removes two M3 bolts, their nuts, and one printed part.

**17 separate color regions** let you customize the flag, lettering, and logo spaces with AMS. The counterbores have two-layer bridges for face-down printing.

## Printing

- Import as **one object with 17 parts** and keep them together.
- Print **branded face down at fixed 0.20 mm layers**; avoid adaptive layers at the counterbore bridges.
- Assign blue, pink, white, and your preferred logo color. Bambu may number the parts and require manual color assignment; see the color guide.

No tuned printer profile is included. [Bridge detail](img/bridge-detail.png) · [CAD checks](docs/cad-validation.json)
